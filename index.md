## Quarter 2 Project Reflections

**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic is the geometry of internal
representations in diffusion models, particularly how
these representations evolve across noise levels.
Unlike autoregressive models, diffusion models repeatedly
transform a noisy input, making it possible to study
how information about image content emerges and
changes throughout the denoising trajectory.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would like to investigate whether semantic attributes
of generated images correspond to consistent directions
or subspaces in a diffusion model's internal
representations. Specifically, I would examine how
these directions change across denoising steps and
whether intervening on them can causally modify the
generated image.

**3. What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

One possible change would be to shift from analyzing
representations at individual noise levels to studying
their evolution across the entire denoising trajectory.
This could help distinguish features that remain stable
throughout generation from those that appear only
during particular stages.

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interested in using activation patching,
causal interventions, sparse autoencoders, and
representation similarity methods such as CKA or
principal component analysis. These techniques
could help identify interpretable structures and
test whether observed features have a causal role
in the generation process.
